# Rights and Attribution

This companion is initially private. It is not an IEEE publication, an accepted-paper announcement, or an institutional endorsement.

## Paper Metadata

Paper titles, authors, venues, citation keys, and source links are provided for scholarly identification. The repository does not redistribute the full texts of the 275 cited papers. Historical bibliography records are preserved as metadata, not as an additional endorsed reading list.

## Figures and Institutional Marks

The three figures are copied from the current manuscript. The timeline combines editable text and foreground elements with raster artwork and third-party institutional marks. Original and prepared logo assets are stored alongside their source URLs in [logos.json](figures/timeline/logos.json). Paper-level attribution is in [attribution.json](figures/timeline/attribution.json).

Affiliation verification does not establish permission to reproduce a logo. Download availability, vector format, or presence on an institution's website is not a publication license. Logo permissions remain uncleared; check each institution's policy before public release or publication. A distinct institution may appear once when several equal-contribution first authors share it.

No blanket license is assigned to the manuscript figures, third-party marks, fonts, or source assets. Rights remain with their respective holders. This repository does not redistribute Times New Roman; rendering requires font files obtained under an appropriate local license.

## Release Gate

Before changing visibility, confirm author approval, manuscript-release permissions, institutional-logo clearance or replacement with text, and licensing for repository code and original figure material. Do not treat private storage as permission for subsequent public redistribution.
